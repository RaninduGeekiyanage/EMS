# M01 UI Screens & Pages

1. **Dashboard & Tenant Settings**: Company profile, EPF toggle, general system options.
2. **Branches & Departments**: Tree visualization of departments and branch assignments.
3. **Designation & Wages Board Setup**: List, create, and update wage grades.
4. **Employee Master Index**: Searchable, filterable list by branch, department, and status.
5. **Employee Profile Creation / Edit**: Multi-tab form:
   - Tab 1: Personal Details & NIC
   - Tab 2: Job Assignment & Reporting Line
   - Tab 3: Payment Mode (Monthly / Daily / Hourly)
   - Tab 4: Bank Account & Statutory EPF/ETF details
   - Tab 5: Biometric Device Identifier & Document Uploads
