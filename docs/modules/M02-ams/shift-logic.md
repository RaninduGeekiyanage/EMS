# Shift & Overtime Logic (Sri Lanka Alignment)

- **Standard Hours**: 8 working hours per day, 45 hours per week (Shop & Office Act).
- **Grace Period**: Configured via tenant settings (default 10 mins).
- **Weekday OT**: Worked hours exceeding standard shift length -> 1.5x hourly rate.
- **Rest Day OT (Sundays)**: Worked hours compensated at 1.5x rate.
- **Statutory & Poya Holidays**: Worked hours compensated at 2.0x rate.
