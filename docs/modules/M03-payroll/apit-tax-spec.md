# APIT Tax Calculation Specification

- **Threshold**: Exemption relief applied per latest Sri Lanka Inland Revenue gazette.
- **Progressive Slabs**:
  - Slab 1 (First LKR 500,000 above exemption): 6%
  - Slab 2 (Next LKR 500,000): 12%
  - Slab 3 (Next LKR 500,000): 18%
  - Slab 4 (Next LKR 500,000): 24%
  - Slab 5 (Next LKR 500,000): 30%
  - Balance: 36%
- Configured in database for easy legislative rate updates.
